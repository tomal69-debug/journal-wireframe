Design Notes

• Soft dark theme reduces eye strain for evening journaling
• Mic button is large and centered — thumb-reachable
• Pulsing animation signals active listening
• Auto-save indicator removes anxiety about losing work
• Mood picker is optional and non-intrusive
• Tags are lightweight — add only if you want
• Smooth transition between dictation and typing, no mode switch jolt
• Cream/warm alternative: swap #0f0f1e for #f5f0e8 and adjust text colors
